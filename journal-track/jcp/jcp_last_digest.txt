   Mo Zhou, Stanley Osher, Wuchen Li，2026-08-05

   Yuxuan Ma, Chunxiao Yu, Yiming Chen, Da-Yan Liu, Brady Manescau，2026-08-04

   https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jcp.2026.115233

4. 超导临界态模型：特性与数值实现
   Sijian Wang, Huadong Yong, Youhe Zhou，2026-08-03

   https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jcp.2026.115273
